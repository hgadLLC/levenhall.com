Risk narrative

Deposits could not be reconciled with the declared business. The account received fourteen cash deposits of 8,900–9,700 GBP in thirty days — 129,400 GBP against filed FY2025 turnover of 48,600 GBP[E-0431] and stated takings of roughly 4,000 a month[E-0447]. The subject offered no explanation for the branch pattern[E-0448]. A seasonal-trade hypothesis was tested and ruled out against the filed accounts.
